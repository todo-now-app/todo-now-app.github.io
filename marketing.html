Have a question? Visit support.

Do I have to connect my calendar?

No. Calendar access is optional. You can use TimeBits for your tasks and routines on their own, or show calendar events alongside them. Your calendar events are used only to display your schedule inside the app.

Can I add a task without choosing a date?

Yes. Tasks without dates stay in Needs Planning. When you are ready, add a date to place a task in your daily timeline.

How do the reminders work?

The Daily Planning Reminder prompts you to review your tasks and events. The Due Tasks Reminder gives you a nudge when unfinished tasks are still due that day. Choose a separate time for each, or turn either reminder off in Settings.

Can I set up repeating tasks?

Yes. Set tasks to repeat daily, weekly, monthly, or on a custom schedule for chores, regular work, and other routines.
